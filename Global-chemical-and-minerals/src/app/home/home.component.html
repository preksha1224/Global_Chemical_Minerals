WHO WE ARE

About Global Chemicals & Minerals

Global Chemicals & Minerals was founded in 2008 by Shirish Pathak and Nilesh Pathak with a vision to manufacture premium quality construction chemicals at factory-direct prices.

Headquartered in Vadodara, Gujarat, we have grown to become one of Gujarat's most trusted manufacturers — serving contractors, architects, retailers, and builders across Pan India.

Our products carry the ISO 9001:2015 certification and our brand "Global" is a registered trademark, ensuring every bag meets the highest quality standards.
